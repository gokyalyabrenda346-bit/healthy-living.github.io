A gentle reminder

You don't have to change everything.

A nourishing meal. A walk outside. A moment to breathe. Sometimes the smallest things make the biggest difference.

Find your small step

Three daily habits

Small steps, repeated every day, make the biggest difference.

Drink water

Aim for 6 to 8 glasses a day.

Move for 30 minutes

A brisk walk counts.

Sleep 7 to 9 hours

Keep a regular bedtime.

Your starter plan

Begin this week.

Eat more colour

  • Add one fruit or vegetable to every meal
  • Swap sugary drinks for water
  • Cook at home three times this week
  • Keep healthy snacks like nuts and fruit nearby

Move a little more

  • Walk for 20 to 30 minutes each day
  • Take the stairs when you can
  • Stretch for five minutes in the morning
  • Try one home workout this week

Look after your mind

  • Take five slow, deep breaths when you feel stressed
  • Switch off screens an hour before bed
  • Spend time outside every day
  • Talk to a friend or family member regularly

Quick answers.

Most adults do well with about 6 to 8 glasses a day. You may need more in hot weather or when you exercise.

A common goal for adults is about 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, such as brisk walking.

Pick one small habit, such as a daily walk or an extra vegetable at dinner. Do it for two weeks, then add another.
